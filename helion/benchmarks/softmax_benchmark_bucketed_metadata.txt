NOTE:
By default, Helion uses static_shapes=True (exact shape matching).
To test true bucketing behavior, modify the @helion.kernel() decorator
in softmax.py. Examples:

1. Dynamic shape bucketing:
   @helion.kernel(static_shapes=False)

2. Power-of-two bucketing:
   @helion.kernel(
       key=lambda x: helion.next_power_of_2(x.numel()),
       static_shapes=False
   )

3. Multiple candidate configs:
   candidate_configs = [
       helion.Config.load('config_small.json'),
       helion.Config.load('config_large.json'),
   ]
   @helion.kernel(configs=candidate_configs, static_shapes=False)
